ABOUT THE SATELLITE FLEET (SIMULATED)

This is a simulated fleet used for demonstration since we don't have
access to a real satellite ground station. Six satellites are modeled,
covering common real-world mission types:

- Aryabhata-Sim-1: Earth Observation satellite in Low Earth Orbit (LEO),
  ~550 km altitude.
- VaruNet-2: Weather satellite in LEO, ~820 km altitude.
- Comsat-Delta: Communication satellite in Geostationary Orbit (GEO),
  ~35,786 km altitude.
- NavIC-Lite-4: Navigation satellite in Medium Earth Orbit (MEO),
  ~20,200 km altitude.
- HeliosScope-1: Scientific research satellite in LEO, ~610 km altitude.
- TerraLink-7: Communication satellite in LEO, ~500 km altitude.

Each satellite continuously produces synthetic telemetry that mimics real
orbital behavior -- for example, LEO satellites swing between hot and cold
roughly every 90-95 minutes as they pass in and out of sunlight, and solar
panel output dips during that eclipse period. Faults are randomly injected
into the data stream so the anomaly detector and dashboard always have
real signals to work with.
